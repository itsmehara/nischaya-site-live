Privacy Notice

Last updated 3 October 2026

Who we are

Nischaya Creative Soft, Chandanagar, Hyderabad, Telangana, India. This notice covers the enquiry form on our website. Questions about it: nischaya.creativesoft@gmail.com.

What the enquiry form collects

  • Your details: name, email address and, if you add it, your mobile number.
  • Your enquiry: your message and any additional details you write.
  • Your plan, if you came from “Start a product”: the package and features you picked, and your answers about your domain, server or hosting, and launch date.
  • When you sent it: the date and time, and the page the form was on.

How we keep and use it

Your enquiry is stored securely and accessed only by our team.

We use it to respond to your enquiry, for our ongoing discussions with you, and to follow up with you about your project in future.

Asking us to delete it

Email nischaya.creativesoft@gmail.com from the address you used in the form and ask us to delete your enquiry. We will delete it from our records.

Other things this website does

  • It keeps simple, anonymous counts of how the enquiry form is used: when a form is started, sent or fails to send, and when our email link is clicked. Each count records only what happened, when, and on which part of the page. It never includes what you type or anything that identifies you or your device.
  • It has no advertising trackers and no cookies of its own.
  • The fonts and the office map are loaded from Google, so your browser contacts Google to show them.
  • Your choices on “Start a product” stay in your browser until you send them with the enquiry form.
